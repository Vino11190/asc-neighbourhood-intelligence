# ASC Neighbourhood Intelligence — Dashboard Evidence

This directory contains selected screenshots from the Power BI dashboard developed for the ASC Neighbourhood Intelligence portfolio project.

The screenshots provide visual evidence of the analytical outputs without requiring Microsoft Power BI Desktop.

## Dashboard Areas

### Neighbourhood Demand
Shows Adult Social Care referral demand across neighbourhoods, including population-adjusted referral rates and identification of higher-demand areas.

### Inequalities
Examines differences in Adult Social Care referral rates across IMD deprivation groups.

The recorded analysis identified approximately a **1.34× referral-rate difference between the most deprived and least deprived groups**.

### Demand & Forecast
Shows historical monthly Adult Social Care demand and the forward-looking demand forecast.

The recorded demand series increased from approximately **980 to 1,295 referrals**, with a **13.9% projected increase over the following 12 months** in the portfolio analysis.
