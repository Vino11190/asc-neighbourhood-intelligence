# Power BI Dashboard

This directory contains the Power BI component of the ASC Neighbourhood Intelligence project.

## Dashboard Purpose

The dashboard transforms the integrated Adult Social Care, population, deprivation and demand datasets into interactive neighbourhood intelligence for strategic planning.

## Dashboard Areas

### Neighbourhood Demand
Provides an overview of Adult Social Care referral demand and population-adjusted referral rates across neighbourhoods.

Key measures include:
- Total ASC referrals
- Referral rate per 1,000 population aged 65+
- Neighbourhood comparison
- Identification of higher-demand areas

### Inequalities
Examines the relationship between deprivation and Adult Social Care referral demand using IMD quintiles.

The portfolio analysis identified a recorded referral-rate ratio of approximately **1.34× between the most deprived and least deprived groups**.

### Demand & Forecast
Visualises monthly Adult Social Care referral demand and provides a forward-looking planning view.

The recorded 24-month demand series increased from approximately **980 to 1,295 referrals**, with a **13.9% projected increase over the following 12 months** in the portfolio analysis.

## Technology

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX / calculated measures
- Python-prepared analytical datasets
- ONS population data
- IMD deprivation data

## Portfolio Notice

This dashboard was developed as an analytical portfolio project. Recorded results should not be interpreted as live operational council statistics without independent validation against authoritative data.
